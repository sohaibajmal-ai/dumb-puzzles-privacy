Privacy Policy for Dumb Puzzles

Last updated: 9 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Dumb Puzzles app ("the app") handles. The app is published by Craftbit Studios ("we").

Information the app stores on your device

Your game progress is saved only on your device: levels cleared, coins, brain cells, hints used, stickers, settings, and daily gift and challenge streaks. We do not run servers that receive this data, and you do not need an account to play. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes this progress.

Advertising

The app shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID
  • your IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location
  • information about your device, such as model and operating system
  • how you interact with ads, such as views and taps
  • diagnostic information about ad performance

Google's use of this information is described in its privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy and in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Where the law requires it, for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, the app asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device's settings.

Analytics and crash reports

The app uses two Google Firebase services so we can see which puzzles work and fix problems:

  • Google Analytics for Firebase records how the app is used: for example which level was started, cleared or failed, whether a hint or skip was used, and whether an ad offer was accepted. Along with these events Google receives an app instance ID, your device model, operating system version, language, app version and your approximate region (estimated from your IP address).
  • Firebase Crashlytics sends a report when the app crashes or hits an error: the error and where in the code it happened, the level you were on, the time, your device model and operating system version, and an installation ID that lets crashes from the same install be grouped.

These reports do not contain your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location, and we do not use them to identify you. Google processes this data on our behalf as described at firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Purchases

The app does not currently sell anything. If in-app purchases are added, payments will be handled entirely by Google Play, and we will never see your payment details.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data sharing

We do not sell your data. Apart from the advertising, analytics and crash data that Google processes as described above, the app does not share data with third parties.

Data security

Data sent to Google for advertising, analytics and crash reports is encrypted in transit.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. The date at the top shows the latest version.
